## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-octane collision between midnight cinema culture and artisanal street culinary craft. The aesthetic fuses Neo-Brutalist structural impact—unapologetic scale, razor-sharp edge contrasts, and industrial gridlines—with the tactile sensuality of high-end dark mode glassmorphism. It evokes the sensory environment of a private screening lounge tucked behind an open flame kitchen: raw cast-iron searing, electric projection light, glowing embers, and theatrical drama.

The target audience spans midnight cinephiles, streetwear collectors, culinary hedonists, and patrons seeking experiential dining. Every interface moment is engineered to feel like an opening title sequence—heavy, deliberate, and immersive. Visual hierarchy relies on immense display typography juxtaposed with ultra-refined, technical metadata clusters reminiscent of film slates and production scripts.

## Colors

The palette operates strictly in a deep nocturnal spectrum, anchoring the interface in deep obsidian and iron tones while directing interaction through searing thermal accents.

- **Obsidian Dark Surface (`#0C0D10`):** The primary canvas background, creating absolute void-depth that mimics a blackout theater auditorium.
- **Charcoal Surface Tier (`#14161C`):** Applied to card backgrounds, elevated sheets, and drawer panels to separate actionable structures from the canvas.
- **Molten Flame Primary (`#FF6B00`):** The primary action accent. Signifies high-energy interactive triggers, primary conversion buttons, and active focus states.
- **Crimson Neon Secondary (`#E11D48`):** Evokes projection hazard lights and film reels. Reserved for critical status tags, live seat availability warnings, and limited-edition cult drop highlights.
- **Amber Filament Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Represents gourmet warmth, ratings, allergen badges, and secondary accents.
- **Gunmetal Borders (`#27272A`, `#3F3F46`):** Provides hard architectural division, card frame definition, and grid lines without visual clutter.
- **Pure Text Contrast (`#F8FAFC` to `#94A3B8`):** Off-white display headers for maximum legibility under high contrast, cascading to neutral slate for metadata and auxiliary labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is split into two distinct emotional engines:
1. **The Display Engine (`Syne`):** Brutal, sweeping, theatrical character forms. Set with ultra-tight tracking and compressed line heights to create a poster-title aesthetic. Used exclusively for cinematic burger names, headline callouts, and hero storytelling.
2. **The Precision UI Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and functional. Delivers razor-sharp legibility across digital reservation matrices, ingredient breakdowns, pricing tickers, and data-dense back-office operational panels.

All display titles employ uppercase or strict sentence case with negative letter spacing. Metadata labels, film runtimes, caloric facts, and table slot markers utilize micro-caps tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic celluloid production slates.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model employs an asymmetric 12-column fluid grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column sequence on mobile displays.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins. Editorial sections permit bleed layouts where culinary photography breaks through the grid boundary while accompanying typography locks to the inner margin.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) canvas margin. Complex modules—such as cinema seat selectors and time slot carousels—reflow into horizontal snap-scroll rails.

Rhythm follows a strict 4px/8px modular base scale, utilizing generous vertical macro-spacing (`space-xl` to multi-token gaps) between menu categories to create theatrical anticipation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional soft dropshadows. Instead, depth is synthesized through high-contrast structural layering, edge illumination, and glassmorphic blurs:

1. **Ground Layer (`#0C0D10`):** The foundational substrate. Non-interactive, pure obsidian.
2. **Elevated Surface Layer (`#14161C` with `border: 1px solid #27272A`):** Applied to menu cards, table headers, and layout containers.
3. **Glass Overlay Layer (`rgba(20, 22, 28, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Employed on floating reservation bars, sticky film reel filters, and modal dialogues.
4. **Thermal Glow Layer:** Primary buttons and active badges project a subtle amber/orange ambient radiance: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.35)`. Danger alerts and exclusive seat lock indicators utilize a crimson projection glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs controlled, low-radius curvature (`roundedness: 1`). Core components carry a baseline border-radius of `0.25rem` (4px), extending to `0.5rem` (8px) for larger menu cards and modal overlays.

This structural sharpness underscores the raw, industrial tone of a smash kitchen while retaining ergonomic micro-affordances. Fully rounded pill-shapes are exclusively reserved for transient status indicators (e.g., "SOLD OUT", "4K LASER SCREENING", "NOW PLAYING") to differentiate metadata flags from structural interactive touch targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic:** Background `#FF6B00`, text `#0C0D10`, bold weight, uppercase, `border-radius: 4px`. Resting state carries an amber ambient glow. Hover triggers an upward 1px translate and intensified edge radiance.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #3F3F46`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, border transitions to `#FF6B00` with text color shift.
- **Crimson Blockbuster Action:** Background `#E11D48`, text `#FFFFFF`, for instantaneous checkout or VIP reservation locks.

### Menu & Screening Cards
- Constructed with surface `#14161C`, framed in `1px solid #27272A`.
- Images utilize high-contrast theatrical food lighting with a 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio.
- Header typography in `Syne` with inline ember tags. Cards elevate on hover with a `border-color: #3F3F46` shift.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), `0.5rem` vertical and horizontal padding, featuring high-contrast monospaced uppercase labels (`label-sm`).
- **Screening Spec Badges:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid #3F3F46`, conveying technical cinema credentials ("DOLBY ATMOS", "DOUBLE PATTY 180G").

### Segmented Controls & Slot Selectors
- Tab strips and screening time pickers rest inside an inset slate trough (`#0C0D10` border `1px solid #27272A`).
- Active items fill with `#14161C`, bound by a crisp highlight border (`#FF6B00` or `#FFFFFF`) with glowing text contrast.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Charcoal container (`#14161C`), border `1px solid #27272A`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#52525B`. Focus shifts border instantly to `#FF6B00` accompanied by a 2px outer thermal halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square architecture (`rounded: 2px`), border `1px solid #3F3F46`. Checked state triggers a pure `#FF6B00` fill with obsidian indicator iconography.

### Data Tables & Admin Matrix
- Engineered for rapid kitchen display systems (KDS) and ticketing management.
- Compact cell paddings (`space-sm` to `space-md`), hairline horizontal dividers (`#27272A`), alternating row fills (`#0C0D10` to `#14161C`), and monospace-aligned numeric cells for ticket IDs, seat coordinates, and revenue values.
